Security

Security that's built in, not bolted on

Here's exactly what protects your account today — no vague promises.

Hashed passwords

Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never as readable text.

Protected sessions

Login cookies can't be read by scripts and travel only over HTTPS. Every change is checked against cross-site forgery.

Brute-force limits

Repeated login, sign-up and verification attempts are slowed down automatically.

Checked attachments

Files are checked to confirm they really are the type they claim to be.

Private by default

Only people in a conversation can open its attachments. Every mailbox is separate.

Safe email display

Formatted emails open in a locked frame: scripts never run, and outside images stay hidden until you choose to show them.

Forward control

Switch off forwarding while you write, and the person you sent it to can't pass it on.

Copy protection

Text copied from an email pastes as ***** instead of your words.

Screenshot alerts

Screenshots are blocked in the Android app, and on the website you're alerted when someone tries. A photo taken with another phone can't be stopped.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Create an account and explore — it's free.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Add it under My domains, publish one TXT record at your DNS provider, and once it's verified you can create up to 10 addresses on it. Today, addresses on your domain send and receive mail between DoMailit accounts; mail to Gmail, Outlook and other providers is sent from your main DoMailit address.

How does domain verification work?

We give you a TXT record for _domailit.yourdomain containing a unique code. When you click Check now, DoMailit looks the record up on public DNS servers. If the code matches, your domain is verified. A domain can be verified by only one DoMailit account.

Is DoMailit secure?

Passwords are hashed, login cookies are protected and HTTPS-only, requests are guarded against cross-site forgery, repeated login attempts are rate-limited, attachments are checked and private to the conversation, and formatted emails open in a locked frame where scripts can't run. DoMailit doesn't offer end-to-end encryption.

Can I manage multiple domains?

Yes — up to 3 domains per account, each with up to 10 addresses. Every domain is verified separately.

Can I send attachments?

Yes. Attach up to 5 files of 10 MB each: JPG, PNG, GIF, PDF, Word (DOC, DOCX) and text files. Messages to outside providers can carry up to 25 MB of attachments in total. Attachments aren't saved with drafts.

How does email authentication work?

SPF, DKIM and DMARC are DNS records that help receiving servers confirm a message really comes from the domain it claims. DoMailit sends mail to outside providers through Resend from its own sending domain. Setting up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your custom domain isn't available yet.

Can I receive email from Gmail or Outlook?

Yes. Mail from Gmail, Outlook and other providers arrives at your @domailit.com address, with the sender's name and any images or attachments.

Can I call someone from DoMailit?

Yes. When another DoMailit user is online, the call buttons on their email light up and you can start a voice or video call over the internet. When the call ends, you both get an email saying how long it lasted.

Can I stop people forwarding or copying my email?

Yes. Turn off "Allow forwarding" while you write, and the recipient sees "Feature disabled by the mail owner" if they try. Copied text pastes as stars. Screenshots are blocked in the Android app and you're alerted when someone tries on the website. Nothing can stop a photo taken with another phone, so don't send anything you'd never want shared.

Is there a free account?

Yes, creating an account is free. Choose a regular account that doesn't expire, or a temporary account that's deleted with its messages after 15 or 30 days.
