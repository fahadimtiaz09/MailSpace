This browser

You stay logged in here for up to 7 days. Log out on shared computers.

  • Your password is stored only as a secure hash — never as plain text.
  • Your login cookie can't be read by scripts, and every change you make is protected against cross-site requests.
  • Repeated failed logins are slowed down automatically.
  • Attachments are checked to make sure they really are the file type they claim to be, and only people in the conversation can download them.
  • Messages are shown as plain text, so scripts or tracking code inside an email can't run.
