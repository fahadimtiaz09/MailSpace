Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: we keep only what DoMailit needs to work. We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings.

Who we are

DoMailit (domailit.com) is an email service with calls and privacy controls, run by its founders Fahad Imtiaz and Nimra Noor. Questions about this policy: support@domailit.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your username and address, your password (stored only as a secure hash, never as plain text), and, if you add them, a display name, a profile photo, a recovery email and a security question (the answer is also stored as a hash).
  • Your mail: the emails you send, receive and save as drafts, with their attachments, and the settings you choose for them (such as whether forwarding is allowed).
  • Your domains: domains you connect and the addresses you create on them.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, and for how long. The call itself (your voice and video) goes directly between the two devices and is not recorded or stored by DoMailit.
  • Online status: while DoMailit is open, it tells our server every few seconds that you are online, so others can call you. You can hide this in Settings → Privacy.
  • Screenshot alerts: when someone tries to take a screenshot of an email, the sender gets an alert email naming that person and the email.
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, an address for your browser or phone that lets us send it notifications.
  • Blocked addresses and reports: the addresses you block, and the emails you report to us with the reason you give, so our team can review them. A reported email is kept until it has been reviewed.
  • Contact form: the name, email address and message you send us.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details in our server logs, used to keep the service running and to block abuse (for example, too many login attempts).

How we use it

  • To run DoMailit: deliver your mail, connect calls, show notifications and keep you logged in.
  • To keep accounts safe: limit login attempts, recover your account, and stop spam and abuse.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your emails for advertising, we don't build advertising profiles, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Services that help us run DoMailit

  • MongoDB Atlas stores the database (on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, India).
  • Contabo hosts our server (in India).
  • Resend sends and receives email between DoMailit and other providers such as Gmail and Outlook.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and your browser's push service deliver notifications.
  • Google STUN servers help two devices find each other to start a call.

These services handle your information only to provide their part of DoMailit.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie to keep you logged in and one to protect forms from forged requests. Your browser also remembers small preferences, such as light or dark mode. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and mail until you delete them. Emails you delete from Trash are removed. Temporary accounts end on their expiry date. When you delete your account, we remove your emails, attachments, domains, addresses, photo, calls and notifications. We keep only your address, so nobody else can sign up with it and receive mail meant for you. People you emailed keep their own copies.

Your choices

  • Change your name, photo, password and recovery details in Settings.
  • Hide your online status in Settings → Privacy.
  • Turn notifications on or off for each browser or phone.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.
  • Ask us anything about your data at support@domailit.com.

Security

Connections to DoMailit use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, attachments are private to the conversation, and formatted emails open in a locked view where scripts can't run. No service can promise perfect security, and DoMailit doesn't offer end-to-end encryption, so please don't send anything you could never risk being seen.

Children

DoMailit isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For big changes, we'll also tell you by email or in the app.
