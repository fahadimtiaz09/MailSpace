Made to land in the inbox

Every MailSpace domain is set up with the three standards that email providers check before deciding where a message goes.

SPF DKIM DMARC

Example of what Gmail shows for an authenticated message:

Authentication-Results: mx.google.com;
   spf=pass smtp.mailfrom=yourdomain.com
   dkim=pass header.d=yourdomain.com
   dmarc=pass header.from=yourdomain.com
