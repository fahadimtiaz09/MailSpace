🌐 My domains

Use your own domain with MailSpace. First, prove that you own it by adding one DNS record.

Where do I add the record?
Add it where your domain's DNS is managed:
• Namecheap: Domain List → Manage → Advanced DNS → Add New Record → TXT Record
• Netlify DNS: Domains → your domain → DNS settings → Add new record → TXT
In the Host / Name box type only _mailspace, without your domain name.
